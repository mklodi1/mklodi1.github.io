I'm Mudassir. Broadly, my expertise is in biomedical data science, machine learning, applied statistics, and neuroscience.

I completed my Ph.D. in Quantitative Biomedicine at Rutgers University - New Brunswick, where I integrated and applied statistical methods to large-scale multi-omics data to predict candidate genes for complex genetics disorders, with a focus on neurological disorders such as Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). Prior to Rutgers, I earned my B.S. in Bioinformatics at Virginia Commonwealth University.

As a Machine Learning intern at EIMplus, I worked on two projects: 1) options pricing and market risk engine tool, implementing my personal trading strategy 2) RAG-system and LLM agent to automate scientific literature review.
